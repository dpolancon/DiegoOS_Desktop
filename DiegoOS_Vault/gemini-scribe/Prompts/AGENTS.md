# AGENTS.md

This file provides context about this Obsidian vault for AI agents.

## Vault Overview

This vault functions as a sophisticated personal knowledge management system, primarily supporting academic and professional pursuits such as dissertation work, job applications, and research projects. It is deeply integrated with AI agents, serving as a central hub for managing tasks, tracking project progress, and generating content, reflecting a highly structured and systematic approach to information and workflow management.

## Organization

The vault employs a highly structured, multi-layered organizational system, reflecting a systematic approach to knowledge and project management. It is primarily divided into three active layers and several supporting folders:

-   **01_Peripheral_Layer**: Contains transient and system-level information, including daily and weekly logs (`A_Daily_Logs`, `B_Weekly_Logs`), friction logs, system diagnostics, and crucial system guides like `AI_Operational_Guidelines` and `diegoos_architecture`.
-   **02_Inner_Layer**: Houses core knowledge and ongoing academic work, such as conceptual notes, dissertation chapters, and job hunting materials.
-   **03_Outer_Layer**: Dedicated to active projects and 'missions,' including specific job applications and research proposals, along with dashboards for oversight.

Supporting folders include:
-   **_Cold_Storage**: An extensive archive for less active or completed projects, areas, deliverables, and skills, maintaining its own internal layered structure.
-   **_templates**: Provides standardized templates for various note types, ensuring consistency across the vault.
-   **08_INBOX**: Serves as a temporary holding area for new, unprocessed notes.
-   **gemini-scribe** and **qwen2.5_OllamaLocal**: Dedicated directories for managing AI agent sessions, prompts, and background tasks, underscoring the vault's deep integration with AI workflows.
-   **system**: Contains backups, scripts, and system-level reports, indicating a focus on automation and vault maintenance.

Notes are organized by project, topic, and temporal relevance, with a strong emphasis on internal linking and adherence to defined system protocols, such as those outlined in `ingestion_protocols` and `CARD_SCHEMA_NORMALIZATION_REPORT`.
