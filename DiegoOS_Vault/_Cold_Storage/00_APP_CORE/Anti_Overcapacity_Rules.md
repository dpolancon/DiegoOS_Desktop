# Anti-Overcapacity Rules

## Rule 1 — One protected deliverable per day

If a second output deliverable appears, either replace the first or park the second.

## Rule 2 — Three moves max

A day has three execution slots. No hidden fourth slot.

## Rule 3 — Parking is success

A parked idea is preserved without being allowed to hijack the day.

## Rule 4 — Closure beats expansion

Every day ends with one line:

> What became clearer?

## Rule 5 — No design during deployment

For the first 5 days, use the vault. Do not redesign it.
