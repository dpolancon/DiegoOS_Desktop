# DiegoOS Constitution

DiegoOS is a local-first Obsidian operating system designed to enhance Diego's own Notion-like capacities.

Its purpose is to convert over-capacity and cognitive overload into constrained execution, without shrinking the programmatic horizon.

## Master principle

Build for Diego. Structure for portability. Do not design for strangers.

## Core operating rule

No structure is valid unless it produces one of the following:

1. A clearer decision
2. A sharper concept
3. A deliverable pathway
4. Reduced maintenance friction

## Stage 1 constraint

The vault begins as a cockpit, not a full city.
