# AGENTS.md

This file provides context about this Obsidian vault for AI agents.

## Vault Overview

This vault functions as a highly structured, systems-driven personal operating system designed for managing academic output, career progression, and cognitive capacity. It specifically coordinates academic job applications (postdoctoral and teaching positions), economics and econometrics research, AI-assisted writing workflows, and personal productivity metrics.

By leveraging strict capacity rules and a rigorous review cycle, the vault balances active project execution with long-term skill acquisition and mental well-being.

## Organization

The vault is organized using a strict, customized numbering system reminiscent of the PARA method, augmented by explicit governance, capacity management, and skill-development folders:

- **`00_APP_CORE/`**: Contains the system's governing rules, including the "Constitution" and "Anti_Overcapacity_Rules" to manage cognitive load.
- **`01_PROJECTS/` & `04_PROJECTS/`**: Houses active high-level initiatives, such as the `DISSERTATION_DEFENSE_WINDOW` and fiscal policy research (`politica`) or AI teaching initiatives (`teachingIA`).
- **`02_AREAS/`**: Focuses on long-acting commitments, particularly the `Application_Pipeline` (tracking job applications using Kanban-style cards) and `TRAINING_HEALTH` tracking.
- **`03_DELIVERABLES/`**: Contains tactical output artifacts, including literature reviews (e.g., Stiglitz et al.), AI prompt frameworks (`gem_camila`, `gem_editing`), and job application core assets (CV, Cover Letter, statements).
- **`05_ACTIONS/`**: Serves as a temporal tracking layer for daily and weekly action logs (e.g., `04-05-2026`, `11-05-2026`), facilitating immediate task execution.
- **`06_REVIEW_RING/` & `07_CAPACITY_POOL/`**: Quality-control and load-balancing zones designed to log system friction and park low-priority tasks to prevent burnout.
- **`08_SKILLS/`**: Houses persistent capabilities and guidelines, particularly professional editing resources (e.g., `Editorial Laws`, `Negative Structural Rules`) to maintain writing quality.

## Key Topics

- **Academic Career Progression**: Managing postdoc and teaching applications via the `Application_Pipeline` (e.g., LSE, TUe, UAH) and tracking them through a dedicated Kanban/Dashboard system.
- **Economics & Econometrics**: Academic research preparation, including editing standards (`iza-nikolov-technical-standards`), econometrics lexicons, and literary reviews on unbalanced growth and debt rollover (Stiglitz et al.).
- **AI Engineering & Prompting**: Designing prompts and tactical frameworks for AI agents (such as `Gem_Prompt` and `prompt_gema`) to assist in editing and tutoring workflows.
- **Productivity Systems & Capacity Management**: Active enforcement of workload boundaries using daily/weekly logging, friction logs, and capacity pool mechanisms.
- **Public Policy & Fiscal Research**: Analysis of fiscal adjustment and political strategies, specifically focused on the Uruguayan Frente Amplio (`politica`).
- **Editorial Standards & Writing Workflows**: Systematic application of strict writing rules, negative structural constraints, and editing memos housed in the `08_SKILLS` directory.

## User Preferences

The user has a highly analytical, rigorous, and system-oriented mindset. Notes are structured with strict nomenclature, numerical prefixes, and a high degree of modularity. They prefer formal, precise, and objective language, with a strong focus on structural integrity and operational boundaries.

The system is bilingual: Spanish is used for political research (`politica`) and specific tutoring workflows (`gem_camila`), while English is used for academic economic editing, post-doctoral pipelines, and system core files. The agent must match the language of the specific directory or file it is interacting with.

## Custom Instructions

- **Respect Capacity Limits**: Before suggesting new tasks or project expansions, respect the framework in `00_APP_CORE/Anti_Overcapacity_Rules` and log systemic friction in `06_REVIEW_RING/Soft_Friction_Log` if requested.
- **Bilingual Context Preservation**: Maintain Spanish for files within `04_PROJECTS/politica/` and `03_DELIVERABLES/gem_camila/`. Use formal, academic English for `03_DELIVERABLES/gem_editing/`, `08_SKILLS/Editing/`, and job application files.
- **Strict Adherence to Templates**: When generating new deliverables, daily action notes, or project logs, strictly follow the corresponding layouts in `_templates/` (e.g., `Template_Today`, `Template_Project`).
- **Maintain Technical and Editorial Writing Standards**: When modifying econometric or academic editing files, adhere closely to the conventions outlined in `iza-nikolov-technical-standards`, `iza-nikolov-writing-rules`, and the files in `08_SKILLS/Editing/` (such as `Negative Structural Rules` and `Editorial Laws`).
