## Stage 1 rule

For the first 5 days, do not redesign the vault.

Use the system before improving the system.

## Daily sequence

1. Define one protected deliverable in [[2026-04-30]].
2. Add max three moves.
3. Put every extra idea in [[07_CAPACITY_POOL/PARK_HERE]].
4. Close the day with one line.

## Forbidden during Stage 1

- No new folders
- No new dashboards
- No agents
- No architecture revisions
- No more than one protected deliverable per day
