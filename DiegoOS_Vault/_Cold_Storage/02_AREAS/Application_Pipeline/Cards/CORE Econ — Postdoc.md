## Fit Architecture

Core fit: Political economy + economics education + curriculum innovation.

Research bridge: The dissertation should be framed as inequality-related through functional distribution, profitability, productive-capacity formation, macroeconomic measurement, and the institutional categories through which inequality becomes visible in policy debates. Avoid presenting the research as only a technical replication project or only a capacity-utilization project.

Teaching bridge: Strong fit through BA-level economics teaching, political economy, applied econometrics, public-policy-facing instruction, research design seminars, data literacy, thesis supervision, and experience teaching heterogeneous audiences.

Pedagogical innovation bridge: Strong fit through ability to build structured learning materials, translate complex economic arguments into accessible modules, use digital tools, develop assessment rubrics, and design reproducible research workflows for students.

CORE-specific pitch: The application should emphasize problem-based economics, empirical grounding, accessibility, policy relevance, and the integration of inequality research into teachable economic reasoning.

Stone Centre pitch: The application should avoid overstating prior Stone Centre ties. Safest formulation: alignment with the Stone Centre’s inequality agenda through research on distribution, accumulation, and macroeconomic measurement; openness to UCL collaboration and Stone Centre network engagement subject to visa or contractual arrangements.

## Strategic Narrative

Application thesis:

I am a political economist and economics educator whose research examines how distribution, accumulation, and macroeconomic measurement shape the way inequality-relevant economic relations become visible. The CORE role is attractive because it combines these two commitments: rigorous inequality-related research and the construction of economics education materials that help students reason from real-world problems to theory, data, and public purpose.
