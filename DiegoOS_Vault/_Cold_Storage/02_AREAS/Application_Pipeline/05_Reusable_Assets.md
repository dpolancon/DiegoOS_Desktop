## UAH Reusable Kernels

### Teaching-first kernel

Political economy, macroeconomics, applied methods, and research design can be presented as teaching capacities that help students connect economic theory with empirical evidence, institutions, firms, and policy problems.

### Research-fit kernel

The UAH package should frame the dissertation not only as heterodox macroeconomics, but as a research program on accumulation, firms, finance, capacity formation, and development trajectories, with reproducible empirical infrastructure and comparative-historical depth.

### Local-positioning kernel

The Chile base is an advantage: immediate institutional availability, knowledge of the Chilean academic and policy landscape, and ability to contribute to teaching, research, and public-facing economic debate without relocation friction.

## Policy-Facing Kernel from Southern Voice / CGD

My policy-facing edge is not only disciplinary specialization. It is the capacity to design and operate research infrastructures that connect theory, data, historical interpretation, and publication-ready outputs across methods and audiences.

## Teaching-First BA Economics Kernel

For BA-level economics, research methodology is not an accessory skill. It is a core student competency: students should learn how models are built, how evidence is organized, how institutions shape economic outcomes, and how policy claims can be evaluated.

## Methods / Infrastructure Kernel

Reproducible data pipelines, structured literature systems, and governed AI-assisted editorial workflows are part of the research contribution. They make complex comparative work auditable, revisable, and teachable.
