# Preparation Note: NYU — Assistant Professor, US Politics

## 1. Position Deconstruction

### What the ad is *actually* saying

| Surface Language | Subtext |
|---|---|
| "Outstanding and innovative scholars" | They want someone with a distinctive intellectual identity, not a derivative dissertation. The writing sample and research statement must signal a *unique voice*. |
| "Future disciplinary leaders" | This is the most important phrase in the ad. They are not hiring a competent junior scholar; they are hiring someone they believe will be a *star* in 10 years. Your research pipeline must project scale and ambition. |
| "US politics" | Hard subfield constraint. Your application must be legible as US Politics to a search committee that likely includes scholars of American Political Development, Political Behavior, Institutions, or Public Policy. |
| "Steps you have taken (or plan to take)" | This is an *action* prompt, not a *values* prompt. Generic DEI language will be filtered out. They want a list of concrete behaviors. |
| "Strongly encouraged to supply links to professional websites" | This is a soft requirement. A missing or outdated website will be noticed. |
| "Fullest consideration by October 6" | Rolling review likely begins immediately after this date. Late applications may be read but will be at a disadvantage. |

- **Ranking:** Consistently top-10 in Political Science (US News, QS).
- **Strengths:** Political theory, comparative politics, American politics, political economy, methods.
- **Culture:** Highly quantitative and formal-theoretic, but with strong qualitative and historical wings. Values methodological rigor and theoretical innovation.
- **Notable faculty in/near US Politics:** (Research current roster — look for scholars in APD, political behavior, race/ethnic politics, urban politics, and political economy of the US.)
- **Institutional ecosystem:** NYU Wagner (public policy), NYU Law, NYU Stern (business), NYU Abu Dhabi (global). Cross-departmental collaboration is valued.

## 2. Fit Assessment

### Critical Question: Is your research legible as "US Politics"?

This is the single most important strategic question for this application. The ad specifies **US politics**, not comparative politics, not international relations, not political economy broadly.

**If your work is primarily US-focused:**
- Lead with your US-specific contributions.
- Frame any comparative or international dimensions as *enriching* your US politics agenda, not as the core.

**If your work is primarily comparative/Global South/LatAm:**
- **Honest assessment:** This is a significant subfield mismatch. A US Politics search committee may not have the expertise to evaluate a LatAm-focused dissertation.
- **Possible bridges:**
  - Do you study US foreign policy toward Latin America?
  - Do you study migration, diaspora politics, or immigration policy *in the US*?
  - Do you study US political economy (inequality, labor, financialization) with comparative extensions?
  - Do you study race/ethnic politics in the US with a comparative lens?
  - Can you frame your methodological expertise (causal inference, historical data) as a contribution to US Politics *methods*?
- **If none of these bridges exist:** Consider whether this application is a strategic use of your limited time given the 4-day deadline. A misaligned application to a top-10 department is unlikely to succeed and diverts energy from better-fit positions.
