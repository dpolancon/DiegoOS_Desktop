## Selection Logic

The application should be calibrated around four evaluative dimensions:

- **Heterodox/Pluralist Fit:** The candidate must demonstrate comfort with non-neoclassical frameworks. They must sound like a political economist who can teach "Economics Perspectives" (pluralism), not a mainstream economist who just studies developing countries.
    
- **Substantive/Regional Fit:** The application must leverage the candidate’s LatAm/Chile expertise to speak directly to "postcolonial perspectives" and "colonial legacies in the Global South."
    
- **Teaching Excellence & Inclusive Pedagogy:** F&M is a top-tier LAC. The dossier must signal a deep, authentic commitment to undergraduate teaching, mentoring, and inclusive classroom practices. Research is secondary to teaching.
    
- **Collegiality and Citizenship:** The dossier must project reliability, approachability, and a willingness to engage in shared governance and departmental service.
