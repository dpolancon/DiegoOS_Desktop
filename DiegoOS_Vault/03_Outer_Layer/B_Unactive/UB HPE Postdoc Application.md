## Role Requirements Verified

The position is primarily research-focused. The postdoctoral researcher is expected to join the project team, collaborate closely with the PI, and contribute to the project’s empirical and scholarly outputs.

### Research Responsibilities

- Co-author working papers and academic articles related to the project.
    
- Contribute to data collection, digitization, and quantitative analysis of historical sources.
    
- Participate in the organization of project conferences, seminars, and workshops.
    
- Work independently while remaining integrated into a collaborative research team.
    

### Project Focus

The project studies how immigrants in Barcelona during the Second Spanish Republic became politically activated and integrated despite cultural, educational, and economic disadvantages.

The central hypothesis concerns horizontal ideological transmission: the peer-to-peer diffusion of political ideas and behaviors as a mechanism of political socialization.
