## Main Risks & Mitigations

- **Risk 1: The Rolling Evaluation Bottleneck.** Because the call specifies that candidates will be evaluated as soon as applications arrive, waiting too long introduces a severe allocation risk.
    
    - _Mitigation:_ Prioritize this file directly behind immediate short-term targets; treat the nominal December cutoff as a soft outer bound and aim for an accelerated submission window.
        
- **Risk 2: Geographic Isolation of Research Agenda.** The position requires full-time, physical on-site presence in Guangzhou, but your research tracks international/global systems.
    
    - _Mitigation:_ Explicitly leverage the "International Travel and Research Fund" promised in the compensation package within your research statement. Outline how you will maintain your international network and tie it back to JNU's institutional footprint.
        
- **Risk 3: Misalignment with Pure Micro/Applied Departments.** If IESR leans heavily toward applied microeconomics, a macroeconomic measurement project could be misread as overly abstract.
    
    - _Mitigation:_ Ground your research statement in concrete, empirical datasets, micro-data aggregation techniques, or structural econometrics to show your frameworks are built on rigorous quantitative foundations.
