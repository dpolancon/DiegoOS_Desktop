# SWIFFT / ULB-VUB — Decarbonisation and Justice Chair

## Role

Four-year postdoctoral Chair-holder / Principal Investigator role within the Decarbonisation and Justice Chair, hosted by the SWIFFT Collective and linked to ULB/VUB.

The position focuses on polluter and wealth elites, just socio-ecological transitions, and the political-economic regulation of privileged groups whose consumption, investment, and policy influence sustain carbon-intensive regimes.

## Fit diagnosis

Strong conceptual fit if framed through:

- political economy of carbon-intensive accumulation;
- elite power, class formation, and socio-ecological transition;
- growth regimes, investment, and decarbonisation;
- justice beyond compensation, centered on the regulation of privileged actors;
- historical political economy of state capacity and transition politics.

The best angle is not to present myself as a narrow climate-policy scholar, but as a political economist of accumulation, distribution, and institutional transformation who can build a research agenda on how elite investment power and carbon-intensive consumption constrain just decarbonisation.

## Strategic framing

Possible project frame:

> Carbon Elites, Accumulation Regimes, and the Political Economy of Just Decarbonisation

Core claim:

> Just transition research has focused heavily on vulnerable groups and compensatory policy. This project shifts the analytical center toward the investment, consumption, and political power of carbon-intensive elites, asking how their position within accumulation regimes shapes the feasibility of democratic decarbonisation.
