## 1. Core Scholarly Identity

> [!NOTE] Working Formulation (Editable Strategy Language)
> **Political economist of uneven capitalist development working at the intersection of heterodox macroeconomics, geographical political economy, and historical political economy.**

### Architectural Translation
This identity refuses both pure orthodox disciplinary siloization and shapeless multidisciplinary dispersion:
* **Analytical Backbone:** Grounded in formal heterodox macroeconomics, classical/Post-Keynesian surplus theory, distribution dynamics, and capacity utilization.
* **Spatial & Institutional Grounding:** Extended through geographical political economy (spatial assets, ground rent, urbanization, infrastructure, uneven territorial development).
* **Historical & Structural Realism:** Anchored in historical political economy (state balance sheets, central-bank solvency, external vulnerability, international monetary hierarchy).

---

## 2. Unifying Research Problem

> **How distribution, productive accumulation, external constraints, and spatially fixed assets shape capitalist reproduction and crisis, particularly in peripheral economies.**

### Core Analytical Matrix
Capitalist reproduction in peripheral and semi-peripheral formations cannot be understood through standard steady-state equilibrium or frictionless neoclassical growth models. The unified research program interrogates four interlocked structural nodes:
1. **Distribution & Choice of Technique:** How functional income distribution (wage share vs. profit share) and cost pressures govern the technical composition of capital and mechanization decisions.
2. **Productive Capacity vs. Asset Stocks:** Why physical accumulation does not translate automatically or linearly into productive capacity, and how capacity measurement must reflect structural bottlenecks.
3. **External Constraint & Monetary Subordination:** How balance-of-payments barriers, dollarized financing, and world money hierarchies limit domestic accumulation, truncate mechanization, and destabilize macroeconomic reproduction.
4. **Spatially Fixed Assets & Ground Rent:** How capital flows between reproducible capital and non-reproducible spatial assets (land, urban property), creating asset-price inflation, balance-sheet fragility, and spatial unevenness.

### Chapter Breakdown & Evidentiary Designs

* **Chapter 1: Measurement / Identification of Productive Capacity**
  * *Evidentiary Design:* Forensic national-accounting replication and measurement methodology (BEA / Shaikh GPIM capacity-utilization framework).
  * *Strategic Focus:* Establishes rigorous empirical identification of capacity benchmarks, correcting foundational capital-stock and utilization distortions.
* **Chapter 2: Main Structural Mechanism (JMP Basis)**
  * *Evidentiary Design:* Econometric and analytical modeling of the distribution-conditioned transformation of accumulation into productive capacity.
  * *Strategic Focus:* Formalizes how distributive shifts govern the choice of technique, and demonstrates how binding foreign-exchange (FX) constraints truncate mechanization in peripheral economies even when firm-level cost incentives favor capital deepening.
* **Chapter 3: Historically Specific Peripheral Crisis**
  * *Evidentiary Design:* Reconstructed historical time series, archival central-bank balance sheets, and institutional financial flows (Chile, Unidad Popular 1970–1973).
  * *Strategic Focus:* Analyzes international monetary hierarchy, central-bank solvency, external credit blockades, and endogenous monetary accommodation under structural import dependency.

> [!IMPORTANT] Methodological Guardrail
> The three chapters employ distinct, methodologically customized evidentiary designs. They must **never** be presented as minor parametric variations of a single statistical model.

## 4. Job Market Paper Strategy

* **Status:** `STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION / TO BE LOCKED`
* **Foundation:** Dissertation Chapter 2 / Essay 2.
* **Target Format:** Standalone publication-ready academic article (40–55 pages, self-contained mathematical apparatus, empirical exhibits, and econometrics).
* **Working Title:** *"The Transformation of Accumulation into Productive Capacity: Distribution, Technique, and External Constraint"*

### Core Theoretical & Empirical Proposition
1. **Non-Proportionality:** Accumulated gross investment and physical asset stocks do not automatically translate proportionally into effective productive capacity.
2. **Distributive Causality:** Functional distribution governs technical choice: shifts in the wage share alter the relative unit costs that drive mechanization and capital composition.
3. **Peripheral Truncation Mechanism:** In an open, import-dependent peripheral economy, mechanization requires imported capital goods. When the foreign-exchange constraint binds, the physical capacity-expansion process is truncated—even if domestic real wages and relative profitability dictate capital substitution.
4. **Policy & Macro Consequence:** Productive capacity bottlenecks emerge endogenously from the structural interaction of distribution and balance-of-payments vulnerability, generating premature stagnation and inflation barriers.

### Strategic Delivery Plan
* Must circulate as an autonomous, self-contained paper with its own literature framing, mathematical model, and empirical identification.
* Avoid circulating an unedited dissertation chapter containing internal dissertation references.

## 7. FONDECYT and the Geographical Political Economy Extension

### Conceptual Continuum (Not a Pivot)
The ongoing FONDECYT research is not a thematic departure; it is the theoretical and spatial completion of the accumulation problem:
* **Dissertation:** Productive capital, reproducible assets, capacity utilization, distribution, external constraint.
* **FONDECYT Extension:** Non-reproducible spatial assets (land), ground rent, asset valuation, credit allocation, balance-sheet collateral, financial fragility, and spatial inequality in peripheral capitalism.

### Empirical & Methodological Programme
* **Geographic Coverage:** Metropolitan land-price inflation in Chile (1983–2024):
  * Santiago Metropolitan Area
  * Valparaíso–Viña del Mar
  * Greater Concepción
  * Iquique–Alto Hospicio
  * Puerto Montt–Puerto Varas
* **Methods:**
  * Time-series econometrics (cointegration, structural breaks, TVAR/VAR)
  * Spatial econometrics & hedonic land-price indices
  * Cadastral & geocoded municipal administrative microdata
  * Quasi-experimental evaluations (DiD) of regulatory and transport infrastructure shocks.
* **Author's Formal Role:** Leading theoretical and formal modeling toward a classical/Post-Keynesian synthesis of:
  * Ground rent capitalization
  * Financial collateral dynamics & credit channeling away from productive capital
  * Balance-sheet fragility under peripheral monetary conditions.

### Strategic Positioning Guardrail
* **Allowed:** Movement toward Geographical Political Economy and Economic Geography.
* **Provisional Umbrella:** *"Land, Finance, and Uneven Capitalist Development"* (5-year vision).
* **Forbidden:** Relabeling unqualifiedly as a pure "economic geographer" prior to established geography-department credentials.
* **Approved Formula:** *"Working at the intersection of heterodox macroeconomics, geographical political economy, and historical political economy."*

## 11. Strategic Risks & Mitigations

* **Risk 1: Subfield Fragmentation / Illegible Profile**
  * *Threat:* Search committees perceive candidate as "neither pure economist nor pure geographer nor pure political scientist."
  * *Mitigation:* Rigorously enforce the Lane Architecture. Let the cover letter frame the candidate explicitly for the target lane while the research statement demonstrates programmatic depth across the underlying unified problem.
* **Risk 2: Premature Relabeling as Economic Geographer**
  * *Threat:* Geography search committees reject candidate due to lack of a formal Geography PhD or disciplinary citations.
  * *Mitigation:* Frame profile as *Geographical Political Economy*—an established heterodox tradition with dual citizenship in both economics and geography.
* **Risk 3: Circulating JMP as an Unedited Dissertation Chapter**
  * *Threat:* Reviewers detect internal thesis artifacts, resulting in lower evaluation scores.
  * *Mitigation:* Enforce immediate standalone extraction of Chapter 2 (40–55 pages) with self-contained introduction, literature review, and conclusions.
* **Risk 4: Evidentiary Design Misinterpretation**
  * *Threat:* Committee members assume all three dissertation essays estimate the same empirical model and critique differences in dataset specifications.
  * *Mitigation:* Explicitly state in the Research Statement that each essay employs an evidentiary design calibrated to its specific epistemological and historical target.
